# Recipe Instructions update

1. Back up DailyForm before deploying.
2. Open Meal Planner > Recipe library and click View recipe; confirm ingredients and step-by-step guidance.
3. Generate a plan and click Cook on a planned meal; confirm the correct recipe opens.
4. Create a custom recipe with cooking steps, save it, and view it.
5. Confirm old custom recipes still open with general guidance.
6. Refresh and confirm custom steps and pantry/plan remain saved.
7. Test offline after refreshing once while connected and verify cooking details open.

Built-in steps are generated cooking guidance based on ingredient types, not professionally tested recipes.
