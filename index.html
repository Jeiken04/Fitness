PHASE 2.4.5 · WEARABLES

Apple Health import

On your iPhone: Health → profile → Export All Health Data. Save the ZIP, then select it below. You can also select an extracted export.xml. No account or server upload is needed. Importing a newer export replaces the previous watch import to avoid duplicates; workout logs you entered manually are unaffected.

Large exports can take time and memory. If ZIP import is unsupported by your browser, extract the ZIP and choose apple_health_export/export.xml. Only steps, heart rate, active energy, exercise minutes, distance, and workout summaries are retained; other Health data is ignored.
