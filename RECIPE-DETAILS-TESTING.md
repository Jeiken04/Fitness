# Recipe details update — test checklist

1. Back up DailyForm data before deploying.
2. Open Meal Planner > Recipe library > View recipe; verify prep time, cook time, difficulty and ingredients.
3. Change servings from 1 to 3; confirm ingredient amounts triple and nutrition totals update.
4. Verify changing servings does not alter the saved weekly plan or grocery list.
5. Create a custom recipe with prep time, cook time, difficulty and steps; open it and verify metadata.
6. Open an existing custom recipe saved before this update; verify fallback estimates display.
7. Refresh and verify pantry, meal plan, recipes, workout and nutrition data remain.
8. Test offline installation and cache refresh on Android/iOS.

Built-in timing/difficulty are estimates; serving scaling applies to recipe detail only.
