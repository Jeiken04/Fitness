# Phase 2.5B testing checklist

1. Export a backup before installing. Verify that the old Boxing Studio templates and history remain.
2. Open Boxing; switch among three four-week programs. Load a session and confirm the Studio fields update.
3. Mark two sessions complete; reload and confirm progress persists.
4. Finish and save a short Boxing Studio workout; confirm it appears in the unified history and weekly chart.
5. Save a session intensity and note; reload and confirm they persist.
6. Save an original boxing journal entry; press Refresh history and confirm it appears without invented rounds/minutes.
7. Export a backup and check `dailyform-boxing-programs-v1` and `dailyform-boxing-studio-v1` are present; restore in a separate browser.
8. Verify Nutrition, Meal Planner, Apple Health imports, timers, and mobile navigation still work.

Limitations: Program completion is manually checked (not automatically inferred from the timer); legacy journal records have no round/minute counts. No backend, accounts, or cross-device sync. Weekly analytics aggregate Studio workouts and original boxing journal entries as separate events.
